SplitPanel Panes side by side or one above the other, with bars to drag between them.

How the editor cuts its page into panes: the code, the console, the running program, its compiled javascript. Drag the bar between two panes to resize them, and the editor remembers the new sizes next time.

Written for: anyone changing the layout of the spell app's pages A picture, not live: the editor's main split. A live <spell-viewer>'s panes don't use it

The editor page nests three: two columns, then rows in each. The left one sizes its rows by the mode:

SpellEditor.tsx · the editor page's panes · 11 lines
  • Give it EITHER columns (panes side by side) or rows (one above the other), and their sizes:
    • true: every pane the same size
    • a list, "30%,2em" or ["20%", "*", 100]
    • a number, or a length in px, em or rem: a fixed size
    • %: that share of the room the fixed panes leave
    • *, or left out: an equal share of what's left, so "85%" for two panes means 85% and 15%
  • Each child becomes a pane, a <SplitPane> wearing the panel's look (bordered, padded, light, rounded, scrolling). Write a SplitPane yourself to give one pane a look of its own; a nested SplitPanel is a pane as it is.
  • One pane per element a child draws: a component that draws two side by side makes two panes.
  • resizable puts a <SplitSizer>, a bar to drag, between each two panes. Without it, spaced puts a <SplitSpacer> there, just room.
  • With an id, a resizable panel remembers its sizes as a preference under that id: keep it unique in the app.
  • Sizes are each pane's inline flex, written straight to the page: a drag rewrites them on every mouse move.
