ErrorNotice An error as a message: inline, or over the page.

What the editor says when something went wrong: "Couldn't load Card.spell". It floats over the top of every page, as Notice does for good news, and stays until its close button is clicked.

Written for: anyone changing the spell app's editor, or reading its code A picture, not live: it shows what the editor's editor.error holds

  • It shows what editor.error holds: editor.showError(error) sets it, and its close button calls editor.hideError().
  • It draws the error with <ErrorDisplay>, fixed near the top of the page.
  • <ErrorDisplay> is one error as an error <ui-message>, anywhere:
    • its header (the error's kind), its message, and a spell error's context and params, if any
    • a close button only with onDismiss, which it calls; it never hides itself, the caller drops it
    • autoHide: calls onDismiss by itself, after 3 seconds
    • ErrorBoundary shows one in place of what threw, by default
