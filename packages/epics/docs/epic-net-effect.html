<epic-net-effect> The <epic-net-effect> element: the Net effect label over a short list of what a choice comes to.

A "Net effect" list: what a choice, a reply or a change comes to, in a few bullets. It draws only the label, Net effect: or Net effect (A, recommended):; the list under it is the doc's own.

For anyone reading or writing an epic page's markup, or changing this element.
Its vocabulary (every name it uses): EpicNetEffect.en.ts.
Its component: EpicNetEffect.tsx, with its style sheet (.css) and tests beside it.

Three real ones: a question's recommended option (epic epic-components, J45, shortened), one option's (option="B"), and a plain one with a sentence (epic wwod-spell-ui, J70).

Its parents:

  • Anywhere prose goes: it's a prose element:
    • <epic-item>: among an item's own text, before its cards
    • <epic-answer>, <epic-more>, <epic-reply>: the cards under an item
    • <epic-option>: an option card's pros and cons
    • <epic-field>: a phase's field, or a labelled block
    • <epic-updated>: a change to a phase's plan
    • <epic-section>: an Overview sub-section's prose, or a report's
    • <epic-version>: an earlier version, kept as it was
    • another prose element (a note inside an aside), or plain HTML around it (a <li>, a <div>)

Its children:

  • prose: a <ul>, or a sentence or two in a <p>

Every question, issue, judgement call and decision ends with one (an epic-page rule, Owen, 2026-10-04). A question's gives the recommended option's; each option may carry its own, inside its <epic-option>.

Name Values Meaning Required
option a capital letter (A) The option it's the net effect of: drawn Net effect (A):. Absent: Net effect:. no
recommended present or absent (a boolean) That option is the recommended one: Net effect (A, recommended):, the word in grey. no

A slot is where the element's own children show.

Slot Holds
(default) What it comes to: a <ul>, or a sentence or two.

A part is a piece of the drawing a page's CSS can reach.

Part Is
base The label over the content.
label Net effect (A, recommended):, a line of its own.
option (A, recommended): only with option or recommended.
recommended The word recommended, in grey.
  • Neutral: no colour and no fill of its own (decision Q20). Net effect is bold, in the text's own size and ink.
  • recommended is grey (muted), as everywhere.
  • Its list sits close under the label.
  • It doesn't fold: the item around it does.
  • Claude writes it inside the HTML it passes to spell dev plan-doc (add ... --details, decide --details, details --file, updated): no command makes one by itself.
  • The epic tool turns the OLD hand-shaped markup into this element, so a doc never holds the old shape.
    • It does this in any HTML a command takes: add --details, decide --details, details --file, updated.
    • The code that does it: IncomingHtml and ProseRewrite.
    • Prose edited by hand in a part file isn't passed through it.
    • The old shape: <p><b>Net effect (A):</b></p> and its list.
  • The converter did the same to every converted doc (ProseUpgrader).
  • A label worded otherwise stays plain prose: <p><b>Net effect (once fixed):</b></p> and its list.
  • On a details page it goes as plain prose: Net effect (A): over its list (PlanItem.asProse()).
