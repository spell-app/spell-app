ScopeDetailsPane The details of a scope picked in the Type Explorer: where it is, its members and docs.

The right side of the Type Explorer, headed "Details": all there is to say about the scope picked in its tree. Where it is, its docstring, its members, its spell, and the javascript it compiled to.

Written for: anyone changing the runners' debug pane, or reading its code Live: a real <spell-app>, its debug pane on the Type Explorer

Solitaire's Type Explorer: the "Details" side, on the right of the pane under the game, IS this component. Click a scope in the tree, such as Card under Solitaire's Card.spell, then open its "Members", "Spell" or "Compiled Output".

Live, from the app's element build, which this set's pages load: /element/spell.pack.js. The app package builds it with yarn build:element.

Only the TypeExplorer draws it, made afresh each time a scope is picked:

TypeExplorer.tsx · the Details side · 17 lines
  • At its top, breadcrumbs of where the scope is in the tree: click one to pick it.
  • Then its docstring, drawn as Markdown. Click it to edit it, when the explorer can save it (in VS Code); never in a <spell-app>.
  • Then its members: click one to pick it in the tree, or its arrow to show its own details right there. As declared, under one "Members" heading, or in alphabetical order, under "Properties", "Actions" and so on.
  • Then "Spell", with where it's declared at the right (a link to open it), the rules its statement made, and "Compiled Output".
  • Every heading starts closed, and stays open or closed alike for every scope: openSections and onToggleSection keep that in the explorer.
  • Its details come in through detailsFor(), asked for with load() the first time they're shown: "Loading…" until then.
  • A SectionMarker marks where each heading starts in a list in document order. The Type and Thing Explorers' trees use it too.
