RunnerConsole One running program's console, in a runner.

What a running program says: each print, and what it logs, a line at a time as they come. A runner shows it on its debug pane's Console tab, or on top for a program with no app of its own.

SpellAppRunner.tsx · the program's console · 7 lines
  • console is the program's own: each <spell-app> runs on its own copy of the spell runtime, so each shows its own console.
  • It reads console once: draw a new one for another, as the keyed above does.
  • Its lines are followed as they change, so a line logged adds a row at once.
  • It draws them as the editor's ConsoleViewer does, with ConsoleLines, and scrolls within its pane, a RunnerPane.
