ErrorBoundary Shows an error in place of what it guards, when that throws.

A guard around one of the editor's panes. When drawing what's inside it goes wrong, the pane shows the error instead, and the rest of the page keeps working.

Written for: anyone writing or changing a pane of the spell app A picture, not live: it draws nothing of its own until something throws, then an ErrorDisplay

It looks like nothing while all is well. When what it guards throws, its default stand-in is an ErrorDisplay of the error, which looks like this, in the pane rather than over the page:

A picture, taken by yarn site:pictures in spell-app.

Each viewer wraps what it draws in one. The input editor falls back to a plain-text editor, and tells the editor of the error:

InputEditor.tsx · the code editor, guarded · 7 lines
  • WHY every pane wants one: an error nobody catches stops Solid updating the WHOLE page, not just the pane.
  • It's Solid's <Errored>, with the app's defaults: an ErrorDisplay of the error, unless you pass fallback.
  • It heals by itself: when the data underneath changes and its children draw, they come back. fallback is also given reset(), to try again now.
  • onError hears of each error caught, to log it or show it with editor.showError(). It may change the editor's state: it runs outside the drawing.
  • A thrown value that isn't an Error is made into one.
