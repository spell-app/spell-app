  • The bars: <AppMenu>, violet, across the top of a page; <PanelMenu>, purple, on top of a pane. Each is a <ui-menu> holding <ui-item>s.
  • <Submenu>: a left, middle or right section, each at least a third of the bar, so the three line up. With spring, a <Spring> pushes it away from its neighbours.
  • <Spring>: an invisible item that takes up the room left over.
  • <MenuHeader>: a pane's name on its bar, "App" or "Spell Tree".
  • <MoreMenu>: a "..." dropdown of Actions. Choosing one clicks it, so an action works the same in a menu or on a bar. Same-titled items need their own values.
    • stub: a dimmed "..." that does nothing yet, as at the far right above.
  • <ProjectActionsDropdown> and <FileActionsDropdown>: MoreMenus of the project's actions and the file's (create, duplicate, rename, delete).
  • <DropdownLabel>: the label before a dropdown in a bar, "Example:" in the picture (ProjectDropdown, FileDropdown).
  • Each piece's host takes no room of its own (display: contents): style the boxes inside through their parts, never the host.
