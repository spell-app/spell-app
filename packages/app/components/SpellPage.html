SpellPage A whole page's box: its look, and its children laid out down or across it.

The outermost box of each of the editor's pages: the project chooser, the editor and the runner. It fills the window, gives the page its dark background, and stacks the page's toolbar above what's under it.

Written for: anyone changing the spell app's pages, or reading their code A picture, not live: it's the box around a whole page of the editor

The project chooser, the page the editor opens on. The SpellPage is the dark grey box filling the window: the violet menu across its top, then the white card of projects.

A picture, taken by yarn site:pictures in packages/app.

Each page is one, holding its toolbar, then its content, down the window:

ProjectChooser.tsx · the project chooser · 9 lines
  • Its look is a set of switches, each a class on its <div>: bordered, dark, light, rounded, spaced, padded, scrolling.
  • fillWindow makes it as big as the window, whatever is around it.
  • rows lays its children out down the page, columns across it: a flexbox either way.
  • Every other prop (id, style ...) goes to the <div>.
  • The editor and runner pages put a SplitPanel under their toolbar, to split the rest of the page into panes.
