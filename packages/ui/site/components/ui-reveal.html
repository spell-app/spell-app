Reveal A reveal displays additional content in place of previous content when activated. Not ported: Fomantic's circular reveal image coupling -- the reveal has no image look of its own, so put a <ui-image circular> in each content.

A reveal may contain content that is visible before interaction: its visible slot.

Visible and hidden content should be the same aspect ratio.

An active reveal displays its hidden content.

Setting active shows the hidden content programmatically, without hover or focus. Edit a value below to try it.
