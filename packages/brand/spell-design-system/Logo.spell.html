App icon

Corners are 22.5% of the icon’s width; the hat fills about three-quarters of its width. Aubergine hat on white is the primary — it’s how the icon will appear most. White on aubergine and aubergine on lavender are alternates.

Using it well

✓ Give it roomKeep clear space around it equal to the height of the hat’s brim.
✕ Don’t rotate or tiltThe hat always sits upright.
✕ No gradients or effectsUse one flat colour from the palette.
✕ Keep it readableNever fade it or place it on a busy, low-contrast background.
