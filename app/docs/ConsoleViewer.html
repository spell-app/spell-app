SpellEditor.tsx · the Program panel's first tab · 3 lines
  • ConsoleRoot is the pane: a ConsoleToolbar above a ConsoleViewer.
  • ConsoleViewer shows the console of the spell runtime the editor runs programs on, once it's loaded. Its lines are drawn by ConsoleLines, so a line logged adds just its own row.
  • It knows parsed spell: a parsed piece shows as "Match {...}", or "ParseError", and clicking it selects its code in the editor.
  • An error drawing the lines shows in their place, and goes to showError.
  • The toolbar's "Program Output" header, then Clear Console, as an icon.
