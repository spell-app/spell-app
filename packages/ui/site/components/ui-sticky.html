Sticky Sticky content stays fixed to the viewport while its container is on screen. The following examples work best on a large display: on a phone the columns get narrow.

Sticky content attaches itself to the viewport when it is passed, and stays fixed to the viewport until it collides with the bottom edge of its context. Additional static content can exist above sticky content without affecting its position.

The context is the sticky's PARENT, here a stretched <ui-column> (Fomantic uses rails outside the segment, where this site keeps its own columns): Fomantic's context setting (any element) has no CSS equivalent, so put the sticky inside the element it belongs to. Stuck Content Stuck Content

With pushing the viewport "pushes" the sticky content: it sticks to the top while scrolling down past it, and waits at the bottom edge (bottom-offset from it) while it is still below the fold.

Sticky content that is larger than the viewport stays where the page puts it: a position: sticky box taller than the view scrolls with the page until its end is reached, then sticks.

Not ported: Fomantic's scrolling of oversized content against the scroll direction -- the browser's position: sticky does the sticking, with no scroll listener.
