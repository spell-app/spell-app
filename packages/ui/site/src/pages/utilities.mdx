Every utility is a `ui-*` class in `@layer ui.utilities`, injected into the page AND adopted into every shadow
root. Lengths come from the spacing tokens (`em` of the local font size), so utilities scale with their region.
Modifiers use an escaped colon (`ui-split:column`), fractions an escaped slash (`ui-w-1/2`).

`<space>` is `3xs 2xs xs s m l xl 2xl 3xl`; `<size>` is `mini tiny small medium large big huge massive`.
The dashed boxes below are demo placeholders (`site-box`), not part of the library.
