RunnerPane One of a runner's panes: a toolbar of tabs, and what the chosen tab shows.

The debug pane under a running app: a row of tabs (Type Explorer, Thing Explorer, Console), and below it whichever one is chosen. Every runner draws it, in a page and in VS Code alike.

Written for: anyone changing the runner, or adding a tab to its debug pane Live: inside a real <spell-app>, from the app's element build this page loads

The todos example, with its debug pane open on the Thing Explorer. That pane, its tabs and what they show, is a RunnerPane: click a tab to switch.

Live, from /element/spell.pack.js (yarn build:element in packages/app), which this set's pages load.

<spell-app>'s runner draws two: the debug pane, and, for a program with no app of its own, its console on top.

SpellAppRunner.tsx · the debug pane, and a lone console · 13 lines
  • It holds no state: pane says which tab shows, and onPane is told when one is clicked. Its children are what that tab shows.
  • With no onPane, or a single tab, the tabs just say what the pane is.
  • Each tab has an id, a Fomantic icon name and a title: sitemap, "Type Explorer".
  • Its tabs are <ui-item>s in a <ui-menu>: whatever draws a runner loads those tags, as <spell-app> does.
  • It sits in a RunnerSplit, and shows a TypeExplorer, a ThingExplorer or a RunnerConsole.
