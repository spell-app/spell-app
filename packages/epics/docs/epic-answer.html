Its parents:

  • <epic-item>: a question's, after its text and Choices, one at most
  • <epic-version>: an earlier answer, kept in the Original Discussion when the question was answered again

Its children:

Child How many What it holds
prose, slot="title" one at most A title with markup.
prose any number The answer, and why.
Name Values Meaning Required
id an id, d7 An old decision's id (d7), from before decisions became answers (2026-10-04): old #d7 links land on it. Absent on new answers.
title text The answer in a few words (Named palette). A title with markup is a slot="title" child instead.

A slot is a place in the drawing that a child goes to: a child with slot="name" goes to that name's place, every other child to the default one (shown as default).

Slot What goes there
default The answer, and why.
title A title with markup (The <code>x</code> API), in place of title.

A part is a piece of what the element draws in its shadow root (its own hidden markup), named so a stylesheet can reach it: epic-answer::part(header).

Part What it is
base The card.
header Its heading band: the fold chevron, Answer · <title>; a click folds it.
toggle The fold chevron, a <button>, first in the band; only with a body.
label Answer, or the old decision's id (D7).
title The title.
body The answer, and why; not drawn when empty; hidden (until-found) while folded.

A state is a word the element sets on itself while something is true, for styling: epic-answer:state(open). Never written in the file.

State When
open Unfolded: it starts so (page state, never written).

The colours mean the same on every doc page: the whole scheme is on Plan Doc Colours (its source: plan-doc.md, "Colours").

  • Ivory, the colour of Owen's voice: the answer is his decision.
  • The band reads Answer · <title>, or D16 · <title> when it keeps an old decision's id.
  • With no body, only the band shows, and it doesn't fold.

It folds by its heading band: a click anywhere on the band (but on a link or a button in it) folds or unfolds it, the chevron first in the band. It starts open, since it's the text being read; folding is the reader's own and never written into the file. Folded text is still found by the browser's find-in-page, which unfolds it.

  • plan-doc decide <name> <Q id> "answer" [--details html] [--option A]: the answer (its title), the details its body; the question becomes decided and answered
  • decide again: the answer is replaced in place; the old one goes into the question's Original Discussion, and an old decision's id stays on the new one
  • plan-doc add <name> decision "title": a question born answered
  • plan-doc inbox <name> apply, for Owen's pick on a question: answered with that option
  • older docs: the converter, from the old decision cards (d7 became <epic-answer id="d7">)
