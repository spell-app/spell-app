<spell-viewer> Runs a spell project in any page, in its own shadow root.

One tag runs a spell program in a page, with no editor: from the spell server, from compiled files anywhere, or what a <spell-editor> beside it compiles. Its toolbar restarts it, and opens a debug pane of its things, its types and its console.

Written for: anyone putting a spell program in a page, or changing the runner Live: the real element, from the app's element build this page loads

The calculator example, from the spell server, with its toolbar. Click Debug to see its things, its types and its console.

Live, from the app's element build, which this set's pages load: /element/spell.pack.js. The app package builds it with yarn build:element.

A page loads it either way:

  • by itself, its own script, with nothing around it: each <spell-viewer> is a root, as <ui-root> is
  • under a <ui-root> that has Spell UI already (a docs page, this one): the element pack, which shares the page's Solid and Spell UI
By itself · 2 lines Under a root, by the element pack · 4 lines
  • What it runs, one of:
    • project: a project from the spell server
    • src: compiled files, from anywhere
    • editor: what that editor compiles
  • Its script API: run(compiled) and restart().
  • Each loads its OWN copy of the spell runtime, so apps on a page don't share their things.
  • Inside it, the runner's pieces: RunnerSplit, RunnerPane, ThingExplorer, TypeExplorer and RunnerConsole; a view draws as its own element.
  • More, with a page of several: the demo, /demo/spell-viewer.html on the editor's server.
