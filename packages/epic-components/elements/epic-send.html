<epic-send>A details page's Send: where the answer stands, and the round button that sends it.

A details page's Send, in its sticky header: where the answer stands, then a round blue Send. It sends the whole page, partial answers too, as often as Owen likes: the page never locks. Beside them, the draft's save: what's typed and not sent is kept beside the page, and comes back when the page reloads or is rewritten.

For anyone writing a page Owen answers on (a details or choice page), or changing this element.
built in epic-send/ (epic epic-workflow P12; the draft P18).
Its vocabulary (every table below comes from it): EpicSend.en.ts.

Live: the real element, drawn by the epics pack this page loads. Off a details page's own address nothing is saved or sent: Send says so.

details page · the header · 3 lines
  • Once a page, in its sticky header (.spell-page-head), after the h1: it sits at the header's right.
  • No children.

None.

A part is a piece of what the element draws in its shadow root (its own hidden markup), named so a stylesheet can reach it: epic-send::part(base).

Part What it is
base Its box: the line, then an error when there is one.
state Where the answer stands, in its colour: grey, blue (not sent), green (sent).
draft The draft's save (P18), muted: draft saved 14:04, draft from 14:04 put back; orange when the page server can't take it (the browser still keeps it).
send The round Send (<ui-button>), by the fill rule: a grey outline with nothing to send, dashed blue with changes not sent, a blue outline once sent.
error Why it couldn't send, under them.

A state is a word the element sets on itself while something is true, for styling: epic-send:state(pending). Never written in the file.

State When
