Q20 Themes may write a component's private variation tokens
D19 · Themes may write a component's private variation tokens

A Fomantic variable that themes a VARIATION (secondary menu, styled accordion, basic button) has no public token to set: the variation writes the component's private alias itself (--_ui-menu-active-background, --_button-weight). J9 Which elements take the stack-with attribute

stack-with says what a layout stacks by: the page (the screen) or its own container. It goes only on the elements that OWN a layout, never on their parts.

  • The owners that take it:
    • <ui-grid>
    • <ui-cards>
    • <ui-steps>
    • <ui-form>
    • <ui-items>
    • <ui-statistics>
  • The parts follow their owner: a <ui-row> or <ui-column> follows its grid, a <ui-fields> its form.
  • Why not on parts too: a part measured differently from its owner mixes two ranges in one layout, e.g. a row in page mode inside a grid in container mode.
  • Each grid works out its range ONCE, for everything inside it (--_grid-range).
  • A row's own stackable, doubling and reversed follow the grid's range too.

Net effect:

  • <ui-row stack-with> and <ui-fields stack-with> don't exist
  • a whole grid always switches at one width

stack-with reuses the mechanism tables already use, proven to work, rather than a new host state.

    • an inherited token, --ui-stack-with, read with @container style()
    • the rules on the root written four times: (@container or @media) times (token or class), each copy guarded so exactly one of them matches
  • Parts read a flag the root works out once:
    • --_grid-range

J15 How a menu's alignment and fluid work

alignment moves the items inside a menu bar that keeps spanning the row; only a segmented menu moves as a whole.

  • alignment="left|center|right" is justify-content inside the bar. The bar still spans the row, so a tab rule or a boxed menu runs across it.
  • fluid: the items grow from their own width (flex: 1 1 auto).
  • A segmented menu IS its items (it hugs them), so for it:
    • alignment moves the whole group (auto margins)
    • fluid spreads the group
  • Emitted as <value> aligned (the shape of Fomantic's phrases).
  • Ignored when the menu is vertical.
  • The other option: shrink every bar to its items and move the bar. Rejected because a tab rule or toolbar box across the row is the usual look for centred tabs.

Net effect:

  • <ui-menu alignment="center" tabular> centres the tabs; the rule still spans the row
J16 How an equal menu sizes its items without fluid

equal without fluid makes every item as wide as the widest, and the bar hugs them. It's done with a grid on the group's root.

  • Applies to equal without fluid or alignment="fluid". The root gets: The root's rules · 4 lines 
    display: grid;
grid-auto-columns: 1fr;
grid-auto-flow: column;
width: fit-content;
  • With fluid, every child is flex: 1 1 0 instead.
  • Why a grid: flexbox alone can't size items to the widest without knowing how many there are.
  • Why on the root, not an inner grid box in a full-width bar: <ui-tabs> draws its tab buttons straight into the tablist (no slot to turn into the grid), and a wrapper would change every tab set's DOM and ARIA tree.
  • Costs:
    • a packed equal tabular bar's rule ends at its last tab
    • a packed <ui-buttons equal>'s <ui-or> takes a column of its own (use fluid)

Net effect:

  • a packed equal group's root is a grid of 1fr columns, as wide as its content
J17 What equal means on each kind of group

equal means slightly different things per family, depending on whether the group already spans its row.

  • Groups that span the row (no fluid attribute): <ui-statistics> and <ui-fields>. There equal means one row, an equal share each.
  • Groups that hug their children: <ui-buttons> and <ui-steps>. There:

    • equal fluid means an equal share of the row

  • Not offered on vertical or circular steps, or horizontal statistics: their children already line up.
