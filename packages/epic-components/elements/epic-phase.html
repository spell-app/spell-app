<epic-phase> The <epic-phase> element: one phase of a plan, in the Phases section.

<epic-phase> is one phase of the plan, in the Phases section.

  • Its attributes hold its id, title, status and estimate; its children are its fields, plan changes and commits.
  • It draws the title line (P2 · Title) with its status icon and the estimate, and folds.
  • Its plan changes are <epic-update> blocks at the end of its description, with no box around them (Owen, 2026-10-11).

Written for: anyone reading or writing an epic page's markup, or changing this element.
Status: built in epic-phase/.
Its vocabulary (attributes, slots, parts, children): EpicPhase.en.ts.
The component: EpicPhase.tsx.

  • The status icon, in the colour scheme: to do, an empty grey circle; under way, a half-filled BLUE circle (Claude is working on it); done, a GREEN circle with a check. A done phase's title reads quieter (muted).
  • The estimate as a badge at the title's end.
  • Plan changes: while it isn't done and its plan changed, an orange pen and the count on its title, folded too. Inside, each change is its own <epic-update> block at the end of its description, with no box around them (Owen, 2026-10-11: "lose the "Plan changes" surround"; before, a Plan changes box atop its body).
  • Its title bar on the row band, edge to edge, sticking under the Phases section's title while you read inside it.
  • Files and Verify stay hidden until the Phases title's toggles show them; commits until the page's git toggle does.
  • Folding: a click on the title folds it; on an epic page it starts folded. With source, its body loads from its part file the first time it opens.
  • spell dev plan-doc add-phase <name> "Short Name" --symptom ... --changes ... [--goal ...] [--files ...] [--verify ...] [--estimate 1-2h] [--before N]: a new phase, todo; a field not given reads TBD. --before N inserts it as PN and renumbers the rest.
  • spell dev plan-doc phase-body <name> <N> --symptom ...: sets its fields.
  • spell dev plan-doc phase <name> <N> todo|active|done [--done ...]: its status, and its Done field.
  • spell dev plan-doc estimate <name> <N> "1-2h": its estimate (the Overview's total follows).
  • spell dev plan-doc updated <name> <N> "..." [--summary "..."]: an <epic-update at summary> block at the end of its description. The changes count is rewritten by the tool on every edit.
  • spell dev plan-doc rename <name> P6 "New title" [--html]: its title (or its title slot, with --html); links that say the old title, and items queued for it, follow.
  • spell dev plan-doc commit <name> <sha> --phase N, spell dev plan-doc commits --backfill: its commits, and commits.
  • Its To review field: rewritten by the tool on every edit.
  • spell dev plan-doc split: source and part-ids.
