C2 The backfill guesses from what it can see

The backfill (yarn plan-doc backfill) marks old items as reviewed by reading past sessions. It can only find what a transcript or the page shows, so it misses reviews that left no trace.

  • What counts as evidence (review-backfill.js): in one of the epic's sessions, you named the item's id in your own message, or answered a modal whose question or answer named it.
  • A session "of the epic": titled with its name, working in its worktree, or saved under that worktree's own project folder.
  • A talk about an item that never named its id isn't found (the caveat below, C7).

Net effect:

  • nothing changes: a backfill marks fewer items than you actually went through

When a review offers to do waiting work now, saying yes moves you to a new window, and the review stops there. You pick it up again with /epic review <name>.
