<epic-option> One option card: its letter and title drawn in its header, its pros and cons its children.

One option card in a set of <epic-choices>. It draws its header (the letter, the title, a thumbs-up when it's the recommended one) and holds the option's pros and cons as its children.

For anyone reading or writing an epic page's markup, or changing this element.
built in epic-choices/.
Its vocabulary (every table below comes from it): EpicOption.en.ts.
The markup rules: PLAN-DOC.md.

Live: the real element, drawn by the epics pack this page loads.

A slot is a place in the drawing that a child goes to: a child with slot="name" goes to that name's place, every other child to the default one (shown as default).

Slot What goes there
default Its pros and cons.
title A title with markup, in place of title.

A part is a piece of what the element draws in its shadow root (its own hidden markup), named so a stylesheet can reach it: epic-option::part(base).

Part What it is
base The card; answered, a panel.
header Its header: letter, title, the recommended thumbs-up, the Choose pill.
toggle The <button> that folds the card or panel (its header's text, the chevron first); a card with no pros and cons: a plain span.
check Answered and chosen: the check before its letter.
title A · A named palette.
recommended The recommended one's violet thumbs-up, after its title, labelled Recommended.
actions While the page is reviewed: the box at the header's end holding the pill.
choose The Choose pill (<button aria-pressed>): marks its letter as the item's pick in the page's review inbox; on the chosen option, solid and aria-disabled: the pick applied.
body Its pros and cons; hidden (until-found) while folded.
tick In an <epic-ask>: its tick box (round for pick one), labelled by its title.
badge In an <epic-ask>: its badge, a small <ui-label>.
more In an <epic-ask more-details>: the (?) <button aria-pressed>.
state In an <epic-ask>: its state icon, state-label its tooltip.
comment In an <epic-ask>: the comment box under it, an <epic-text-box> for <key>-<letter>.

A state is a word the element sets on itself while something is true, for styling: epic-option:state(answered). Never written in the file.

State When
answered Its question is answered: a panel under Choices.
chosen Its letter is its <epic-choices chosen>.
open Unfolded: a card starts so; an answered panel only when it's the chosen one (page state).
picked Its letter is the item's pick in the review inbox, in its card set, not applied yet: framed green: dashed until sent, then outlined.
answering An option of an <epic-ask>: a tick box, a comment box.
ticked In an <epic-ask>: ticked, framed green (a pick is a decision).
filtered In an <epic-ask filter>: done, and hidden while it shows Open.
Event Detail Cancelable When
ui-change { letter: string, selected: boolean, more: boolean } In an <epic-ask>: Owen ticked or unticked it (selected), or pressed its (?) (more). Its question takes it, and draws the result.

The colours mean the same on every doc page: the whole scheme is on Epic Page Colours (its source: plan-doc.md, "Colours").

  • Its header reads A · A named palette; the recommended one adds a violet thumbs-up (violet: Claude's voice), labelled Recommended.
  • It folds by its header (the chevron first), and starts open; a card with no pros and cons doesn't fold.
  • Answered: a panel under Choices. The chosen one has a check before its letter, green text, and is the only panel open.
  • While the page is reviewed, a Choose pill at the header's end marks the letter as Owen's pick.
    • It's green, since a pick is a decision: dashed until sent, outlined once sent.
    • The card is framed to match.
    • On the chosen option the pill is solid green: the pick applied.
    • In an <epic-ask> (a page Owen answers on, epic epic-workflow P12): a card a row, its header a tick box labelled by its title (round for pick one), the badge, then the (?) of "Provide more details" and its state icon at the end; a comment box under it; ticked, framed green; done, hidden under its question's Open.

While an epic page is being reviewed (<epic-page reviewing>, served by the page server), its marks go to the doc's review inbox: the file of Owen's marks that Claude reads (<name>.inbox.json).

Written with its <epic-choices>: the same commands (add --details, details --file), and an old option grid is turned into cards on the way in.

  • The pick isn't written on the option: it's the set's chosen.
