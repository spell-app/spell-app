Custom states: CSS can match them with epic-updated:state(name).

State When
:state(open) Unfolded: it starts so (page state, never written).
  • The fence: a dashed ORANGE box on a faint orange wash, its icon orange: orange means changed since you looked (the colour scheme).
  • Its heading: the fold chevron, the icon, Updated, the time and during P6 in small mono, muted.
  • In its phase: in the phase's Plan changes box, atop its body, on a faint orange ground under a heading that folds it (chevron, pen, Plan changes · 2).
    • The box is open while the phase isn't done, and folded once it is.
    • While the phase isn't done, its title shows an orange pen and how many changes, even folded.
  • Folding: open to start with; a click on its heading folds what changed.
    • Folded text is still found by find-in-page.
    • Whether it's open is the reader's choice, never written in the doc.
  • Nothing any more: spell dev plan-doc updated <name> <N> "<html>" writes an <epic-update> since 2026-10-11.
  • Every edit migrates an older doc first (updates-as-epic-update): each line becomes an <epic-update at phase summary> at the end of its phase's description, and the copies in the Phases section (of) go.
  • spell dev plan-doc add-phase ... --before N: renumbers the phases that move, in both attributes (phase, of).
  • spell dev plan-doc convert: from an old phase's Updated field, one per dated line, then migrated to <epic-update> as it writes its files.
