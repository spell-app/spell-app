2. The next three months

Pieced together from the code and old notes. We settle it in goals, after motivation and audience.

October

Solid ground
  • Settle the why, the who and the look
  • Fix the language's sharpest edges: precedence and types
  • Review and merge the work in flight
  • Publish @spell-app/ui 0.1

November

First spell in five minutes
  • A Mac app to download, with projects in your own folder
  • Getting started, a tour and a phrase book
  • A website with live, editable examples

December

Public preview
  • A signed Mac app
  • Website and docs live
  • Three polished examples
  • Five real people trying it, then tell the world

3. How we work

The dialog loop

Words we use
people
The people who use spell. Never "users".
G goal
Something we want, on one of three horizons: now → December, 2027, someday.
I idea
A way we might get there. Not decided.
Q question
Waiting on a conversation. Each comes with Claude's suggestion, folded.
R risk
What's likely to bite us, and what we'd do about it.
D decision
Settled, with the why. Agents don't relitigate these.
W work
A piece of work an agent (or you) can pick up.
T thought
Your note, waiting to be worked in. Digested ones stay, with what came of them.
app/Q3
Item Q3 on the app page. Ids never change; closed items stay, struck through.

The files
  • goals/spell/<topic>/<topic>.html: the page for people, and the source of truth.
  • goals/spell/<topic>/<topic>.md: the same topic as a brief for agents: context, decisions, work, and the questions they must not decide.
  • /goals, /goals-thought, /goals-update, /goals-open, /goals-open-vs in Claude Code; spell goals ... or yarn goals ... in a terminal. The rules: AGENTS.md.

Topics that may come up
  • website: the home page, download page and hosting, if brand and docs don't cover it.
  • release: versions, changelogs, publishing to npm, signing, CI.
  • community: open source, contributing, licence, where people gather.
  • quality: reviewing what agents wrote, tests, the debt files.
  • sustainability: how spell keeps going: the old SpellCo notes, funding, or none.

Lives in goals/ at the repo root for now. Developer docs: packages/docs.
