Write in plain language.

Clear words. Better results.

Spell understands natural language, not technical jargon. Writing in plain language helps you get more accurate results, save time, and make your apps easier to change — for everyone.

Tip Be specific, not complicated.

Use everyday words and short, direct sentences. The clearer your instructions, the better Spell can understand and act on them.
