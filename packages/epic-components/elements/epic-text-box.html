<epic-text-box>A box Owen types in on a page he answers on: notes, a comment, Other, feedback.

A box Owen types in, on a page he answers on: one line to start, growing with its text. What he types is part of the page's answer, by its type and for. The page's notes box also shows what the page KEPT: text typed for a question, option or section an agent's rewrite took off the page.

For anyone writing a page Owen answers on (a details or choice page), or changing this element.
built in epic-text-box/ (epic epic-workflow P12; the kept text P18).
Its vocabulary (every table below comes from it): EpicTextBox.en.ts.

Live: the real element, drawn by the epics pack this page loads. Off a details page's own address nothing is saved or sent: Send says so.

Written in a page: a section's comment box, last in it; the page's notes, after the last question. The rest are drawn by other elements, in their shadow roots.

details page · a comment box and the notes · 5 lines
  • Written in a details page: last in a section that isn't a question (for its id), and once after the last question (type="notes").
  • Drawn by <epic-ask> (its Other), <epic-option> (its comment, in an ask) and <epic-choices-bar> (the feedback).
  • No children.

A part is a piece of what the element draws in its shadow root (its own hidden markup), named so a stylesheet can reach it: epic-text-box::part(base).

Part What it is
base The box around the text field.
field The <textarea>: one line to start, growing with its text to a third of the window.
kept A notes box's KEPT text, above its field, while there is any: an orange-edged box, "Kept from before the page changed", then each text.
kept-item One kept text: its old title (the id it was under on hover), what was typed, and a × to drop it.

A state is a word the element sets on itself while something is true, for styling: epic-text-box:state(filled). Never written in the file.
