Sidebar A sidebar hides additional content beside a page. Types

A sidebar: <ui-pushable> (the context) holds <ui-sidebar>s and one <ui-pusher> (the page beside them).

Although sidebars can be used with any content, in most cases sidebars hold an inverted vertical <ui-menu>. See the Usage tab for more details. 1 2 3 Toggle Home Topics Friends Toggle States

A sidebar can be visible on the page: add visible.

A modal sidebar that starts visible takes focus as the page loads, so a sidebar shown from the start is usually persistent: part of the page, nothing dimmed or blocked. Alpha Beta Gamma

The pusher is the element pushed away by the sidebar: it holds your site content. A pusher is dimmed while a modal sidebar beside it is visible.

Not ported: a dimmed pusher of its own -- the pushable dims (and makes inert) its pusher exactly while a modal sidebar shows, so the two never disagree. 1 2 Show Variations Left Right Top Bottom One Two One Two One Two One Two

A sidebar can specify its width: width is very thin (60px), thin (150px), 260px by default, wide (350px) or very wide (475px), or columns of the viewport (4, 1/4, 25%).

A sidebar adjusts its animations to any width, the words' or your own --ui-sidebar-width. Very thin Thin Default Wide Very wide A quarter Thin Default Wide Very wide A quarter One Toggle More Examples

Not all sidebar transitions are available for every sidebar direction: overlay, push and scale down work on every edge; uncover (the default at the sides), slide along and slide out on the left and right only.

Not ported: Fomantic's dimPage setting -- a modal sidebar always dims the page beside it, a persistent one never does. Overlay Push Scale Down Uncover Slide Along Slide Out Overlay Push Scale Down Uncover Slide Along Slide Out

Multiple sidebars can be displayed at the same time when they are persistent, with a transition like push or overlay. A modal sidebar is a drawer: one at a time.
