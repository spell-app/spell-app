<Section title="Behaviour">
  A card is an `<article>`:  a self-contained piece of the page, such as a person, a product or a post.  With `href`
  the whole card becomes ONE link (an `<a>`), named by its text.  `link` alone is only the hover lift, so a card that
  goes somewhere needs `href`.

  Its content is the generic [content parts](../ui-parts/):  `<ui-content>`, `<ui-header>`, `<ui-meta>`,
  `<ui-description>`, `<ui-extra>` and `<ui-avatar>` inside a card take the card's look, so there is no
  `ui-card-header`.  A slotted `<img>` is a full-width image.  The shorthand attributes `image`, `header`, `meta`,
  `description` and `extra` render the same parts for you, in that order:  the image, one content block, your own
  content, then the extra content.  Slot a part of the same kind (your own `<ui-header>`, or an `<img>` for `image`)
  and that shorthand steps aside.

  A `<ui-cards>` group hands its variations to its cards:  `size`, `color`, `horizontal`, `raised`, `link`, `basic`
  and `inverted` on the group apply to every card that doesn't set its own.  `columns` sets how many cards share a
  row;  without it each card keeps a fixed width and the row wraps.  `doubling` and `stackable` follow the group's
  own width, not the window's:  a group narrower than 768px shows fewer cards to a row (`doubling`) or one
  (`stackable`), so a group in a narrow column stacks even on a wide screen.

  Cards fire no events of their own.

</Section>

<Section title="Theming">
  Theme a card with the public `--ui-card-*` tokens:  set one on the element, on any ancestor (a `<ui-cards>`
  group, a region, `:root`), or on the card's box through `::part(card)`.  A group's spacing tokens,
  `--ui-cards-*`, go on the group or above it.  The shorthand blocks are parts too:  `image`, `content`, `header`,
  `meta`, `description` and `extra`.

  The look of the parts inside (`--ui-card-header-color`, `--ui-card-content-padding`, `--ui-card-meta-color`
  ...) is themed the same way, on the card or above it (see the content parts page).

```css
ui-card {
  --ui-card-radius: 0;
  --ui-card-width: 20em;
  --ui-card-header-color: var(--ui-violet-text);
}
ui-cards {
  --ui-cards-spacing: 2em;
}
```

<Section title="Accessibility">
  - A card is an `<article>`, so a screen reader can move from card to card.  Give its `<ui-header>` a `level` when
    the card's title should also be a heading in the page outline.
  - A link card is one link whose name is all of its text.  Keep other links and buttons out of it:  nested
    interactive content can't be reached or named properly.  A card with its own links (a linked header, an extra
    link) should not have `href`.
  - `disabled` sets `aria-disabled`, and a link card loses its `href`, so it is no longer a Tab stop.
  - `loading` sets `aria-busy` and announces "Loading…" (translatable) through a hidden status message.
  - A `<ui-cards>` group is a `role="list"` and each card in it a `listitem`, so a reader hears how many cards there
    are.
  - The `image` shorthand is decorative (`alt=""`) unless you give `alt`;  a slotted `<img>` needs its own `alt`.
  - Colour is never the only signal:  a coloured card's meaning belongs in its text.
  - If the element can't render, its native fallback keeps the same `<article>` (or link) and class words, with the
    shorthands in place, so the card still looks and links right.  It drops the group's variations on its cards, the
    shorthands stepping aside for slotted parts, `aria-busy` and the loading announcement.
</Section>
