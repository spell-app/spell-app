In Debug, the editor's page draws it as TreeRoot, without its toolbar, in the Program panel's last tab:

SpellEditor.tsx · the Program panel's last tab · 3 lines
  • TreeRoot is the pane: a TreeToolbar (just its "Spell Tree" header today) above a TreeViewer. It follows the open file's parse and the editor's cursor.
  • TreeViewer works out the tree of just the line under the cursor, so it stays small, and draws it with Spell UI's <ui-tree-diagram>. A new tree on every move.
  • With no line there, or nothing parsed yet, it says to put the cursor on a line.
  • An error working out the tree shows in its place and goes to showError; the next move tries again.
