Dimmer A dimmer hides distractions to focus attention on particular content. Types

A simple dimmer displays no content. It covers its parent; active shows it, and a click on the dimmer hides it. The buttons show and hide it with command="--show" / "--close".

Overlayable Section

A dimmer can display content, centred over what it dims. A dark dimmer is the dark scheme for its content, so a plain header reads light.

Overlayable Section Dimmed Message!

A dimmer can be formatted to be fixed to the page: page covers the whole viewport, as a modal dialog, so the page behind it can't be reached. A click anywhere, or Escape, brings the page back.

A dimmer can blur its contents: blurring blurs and greys what's behind it.

Fomantic puts blurring on the dimmable parent (ui blurring segment); ours goes on the dimmer, which blurs through backdrop-filter. Title Add View Title Add View

A dimmer can be controlled without JavaScript. On the element, simple looks the same: active shows it.

A dimmer can be formatted to have a different depth of background shading: shade="medium", light or very light.

A dimmer can trigger a visibility change on an event: on="hover" shows it while the pointer is over its parent, or keyboard focus is inside it (Tab reaches its buttons).
