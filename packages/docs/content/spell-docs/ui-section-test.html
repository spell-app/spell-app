  • Jump to a sub-sub-item: an h4.

Every section of a page is a <ui-section sticky collapsible dividing>: its title sticks while any of it is on screen, folds the section, and has a rule under it. Nested sections stack their titles under their parents'.

The runtime reads the sections into the contents sidebar and the rail, counts the items of each top-level section, sets the top-level sections' offset below the page header, and remembers what the reader folded.

A section's label in the contents is its header attribute, or the text of its slot="header" child. Its icon is its slotted <ui-icon>, or its icon attribute.
